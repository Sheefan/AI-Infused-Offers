# Podcast Guest Bio: Ian Teh

## Show notes

Ian Teh is a Perth-based growth and AI advisor who's sat on every side of the table. He's sold websites and ads, run business development for everyone from local small businesses to large enterprises, and worked across the public sector.

Today he leads marketing, business development and partnerships at Polygroup AI, building bespoke, governed AI that serves the business instead of running it. He also runs marketing and growth at Sheefan Media and hosts The Sheefan Show.

His take: most businesses don't need more tools. They need intelligent communication, and a lot less bureaucracy.
